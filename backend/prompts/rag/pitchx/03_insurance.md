Claim Process:
1. Call our service center or file directly with insurer
2. Take vehicle to our workshop
3. Surveyor inspects (we coordinate)
4. Repairs approved → work begins
5. Vehicle ready → customer pays deductible (if any)
6. Balance settled by insurer directly

Documents required: RC, insurance policy, driving license, FIR (if theft/third-party)

Typical repair time: 3–7 days depending on parts availability
