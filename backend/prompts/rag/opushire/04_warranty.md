================================================================================
# 4. WARRANTY
================================================================================

Standard Warranty: 2 years / 40,000 km (whichever comes first)
Transferable to subsequent owners within warranty period

Extended Warranty Options:
- 3 years / 60,000 km: ₹4,999 – ₹8,999
- 4 years / 80,000 km: ₹8,999 – ₹14,999
- 5 years / 100,000 km: ₹12,999 – ₹19,999
(Prices depend on model — smaller cars lower, SUVs higher)

What Extended Warranty Covers:
- Engine, transmission, drivetrain
- Electrical and electronic components
- AC system
- Steering, suspension, brakes
- Fuel system
- Cooling system

What is NOT Covered:
- Wear and tear items (brake pads, tyres, clutch, bulbs, wipers)
- Consumables (oil, coolant, refrigerant)
- Cosmetic items (paint, upholstery, trim)
- Damage from accident, misuse, or unauthorized modification
- Non-genuine parts usage

Warranty Claim Process:
1. Customer reports issue at service center
2. Technician diagnoses and documents fault
3. If warrantable → parts replaced at no charge
4. If not warrantable → estimate provided → customer approval needed

Out-of-warranty cars can still be serviced at the center — standard service rates apply
