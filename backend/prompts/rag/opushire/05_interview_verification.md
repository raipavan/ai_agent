# Advanced Interview Technology & Verification
- **Avatar-Based Video Interviews**: An AI-driven digital avatar conducts initial interviews. This ensures 100% consistency across all candidates and creates a low-anxiety environment that strips out human bias.
- **P2P Video Interview Platform**: Built-in HD live video featuring real-time collaborative coding environments and on-screen digital evaluation scorecards for panel interviews.
- **Biometric Identity Verification**: Facial recognition and identity document matching occur before the interview begins, completely preventing candidate impersonation and proxy testing.
