# 27-Signal Proctoring Framework
OpusHire's AI proctoring engine monitors 27 discrete integrity signals across five categories — from biometric identity verification to browser-level behavioral analysis. Each signal is assigned a severity tier that drives automated review routing.
Categories include screen control (tab switch, window blur, fullscreen exit), identity & face tracking (multiple faces, identity drift, mismatch), browser/input monitoring, audio integrity (multiple voices, whispering), and behavior & environment (gaze aversion, prohibited items, earpiece).
