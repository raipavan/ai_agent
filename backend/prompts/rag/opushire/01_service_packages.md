# 1. SERVICE PACKAGES — DETAILED PRICING & RECOMMENDED ADD-ONS
================================================================================

**When a customer books a service, also recommend relevant add-ons:**
- Oil change → recommend coolant top-up, brake inspection, cabin filter
- AC service → recommend cabin filter, gas check
- Brake service → recommend fluid replacement
- Wheel alignment → recommend balancing, rotation
- Any service → recommend free car wash

****************************************
1A. PERIODIC MAINTENANCE SERVICE (PMS)
****************************************

Every 10,000 km or 12 months (whichever is earlier)

| Service # | Interval       | Estimated Cost (labor + parts) |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| 1st       | 10,000 km      | ₹2,999 – ₹3,999              |
| 2nd       | 20,000 km      | ₹3,499 – ₹4,999              |
| 3rd       | 30,000 km      | ₹3,999 – ₹5,499              |
| 4th       | 40,000 km      | ₹5,999 – ₹8,999 (Major)      |
| 5th       | 50,000 km      | ₹3,999 – ₹5,499              |
| 6th       | 60,000 km      | ₹4,999 – ₹6,999              |
| 7th       | 70,000 km      | ₹4,499 – ₹5,999              |
| 8th       | 80,000 km      | ₹6,999 – ₹10,999 (Major)     |

What's included in PMS:
- Engine oil change + oil filter
- Air filter cleaning/replacement
- Coolant top-up/check
- Brake fluid level check
- Windshield washer fluid top-up
- Battery health check
- Tyre pressure + condition check
- Lighting and electrical check
- Suspension and steering check
- Brake pad thickness measurement
- Wheel bearing check
- Drive belt inspection
- Road test

****************************************
1B. MAJOR SERVICE (40,000 / 80,000 km)
****************************************
Everything in PMS plus:
- Spark plug replacement
- Air filter replacement
- Cabin air filter replacement
- Fuel filter replacement
- Coolant replacement
- Brake fluid replacement
- Clutch and transmission fluid check
- Full AC system check
- Wheel alignment + balancing
- Engine carbon cleaning (recommended)
- All belts and hoses inspection

Estimated Cost: ₹5,999 – ₹10,999 depending on model
