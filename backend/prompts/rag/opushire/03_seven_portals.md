# The Seven Deeply Integrated Portals
OpusHire operates through seven deeply integrated portals, each addressing a discrete phase of the hiring lifecycle. Every portal feeds structured data into the next, creating a continuous intelligence loop:
1. **Client & Sales Portal (01)**: Secure client collaboration, CRM, deal tracking, revenue forecasting.
2. **Candidate Recruitment Portal (02)**: Resume builder, video portfolio, AI resume parsing & analysis.
3. **Job Fulfillment Portal (03)**: AI JD creation, omnichannel job broadcasting, SEO optimization.
4. **Candidate Sourcing Portal (04)**: Boolean AI search, X-Ray sourcing, referral management, auto-import.
5. **Outreach & Screening Portal (05)**: Bulk AI screening, multi-channel outreach, ranking & scoring.
6. **Candidate Engagement Portal (06)**: Proctored AI video interviews, psychometrics, P2P live interviews.
7. **Pre-Onboarding & Verification Portal (07)**: Biometric identity check, background verification, reference checks.
