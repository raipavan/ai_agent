# Who OpusHire Serves
- **Staffing and Recruiting Firms**: Manage client requirements more effectively, accelerate candidate submissions, and improve recruiter productivity through standardized workflows.
- **Enterprise Talent Teams**: Standardize hiring processes, improve collaboration across stakeholders, and increase consistency in candidate evaluation and decision-making.
- **High-Growth Businesses**: Scale recruitment operations without adding complexity, disconnected tools, or unnecessary manual effort.
