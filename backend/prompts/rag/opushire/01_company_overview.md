# Who We Are & The Crisis of Fragmentation
Modern enterprises face a crisis of recruitment fragmentation: recruiters navigate disconnected applicant tracking systems (ATS), manual screening tools, siloed communication platforms, and disconnected assessment vendors while managing rising application volumes, candidate ghosting, and pressure to reduce time-to-hire.

OpusHire is a comprehensive, AI-native recruitment operating system designed to eliminate this fragmentation entirely. By consolidating every stage of the hiring lifecycle — from job description creation and candidate sourcing, through intelligent screening and video interviewing, to background verification and pre-onboarding — into a single, cohesive platform.
