================================================================================
# 9. EMERGENCY / SPECIAL SITUATIONS
================================================================================

**Breakdown on Highway:**
1. Stay calm, move to side, turn on hazard lights
2. Call roadside assistance: 1800-102-1800
3. Share exact location (Google Maps pin) and vehicle model
4. Tow truck dispatched within 20-40 minutes

**Accident with Injury:**
1. Call ambulance: 108
2. Call police: 100
3. Take photos/videos for insurance claim
4. Do NOT move vehicle until police arrives (unless blocking emergency)
5. Tow to our center for repairs

**Engine Overheating:**
1. Pull over safely and turn off AC
2. Turn on heater to max (draws heat from engine)
3. Do NOT open radiator cap when hot
4. Wait 15-20 minutes for engine to cool
5. Call roadside assistance

**Flat Tyre:**
1. Find safe spot, turn on hazard lights
2. Use spare wheel if available and you know how
3. Or call roadside assistance
4. Spare wheel max speed: 80 km/h

**Key Lost / Locked Out:**
1. Check for spare key at home
2. Call roadside assistance for lockout help
3. New key cost: ₹2,000 – ₹5,000
4. Proof of ownership required
