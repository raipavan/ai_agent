# Training Room Furniture
Complete solutions for training rooms, classrooms, seminar halls and meeting/training spaces.

## Product Range
- Training chairs with writing pads (foldable options available).
- Stackable chairs for flexible room layouts.
- Training tables and pod systems for group learning.
- Instructor / podium furniture.
- Foldable and mobile options for multi-purpose rooms.

## Features to Explain
- Space-saving: stackable and foldable furniture lets the room switch between layouts quickly.
- Pod systems make group training and collaboration easy.
- Durable, easy-to-clean surfaces.
- Capacity planning: we can help estimate seating for the room size.

## Conversation Tips
- Ask the number of seats/trainees and whether they need flexibility (stacking/folding).
- Offer layout guidance or a site visit for larger training centers.
