# Who We Are
Lila Decor & Company is a premier office furniture manufacturer based in Mumbai, India, established in 1995 with nearly 30 years of manufacturing experience.

- Founded: 1995
- Location: Mumbai, India
- Focus: Premium office furniture, ergonomic chairs, lounge seating, training room furniture, cafeteria/canteen furniture
- Serves: Corporate offices, co-working spaces, banks, schools, colleges, training centers, hospitals, cafeterias and canteens
- Languages supported: English, Hindi, Gujarati

## What Makes Lila Decor Different
- Nearly 30 years of in-house manufacturing experience — furniture is made to last.
- Two clear collections: the Make in India collection (premium Indian craftsmanship, great value) and the Imported executive series (international design and ergonomics).
- Full-service support: consultation, site visits, layout guidance, delivery and after-sales support.
- Customization available for bulk orders, corporate branding and special layouts.
