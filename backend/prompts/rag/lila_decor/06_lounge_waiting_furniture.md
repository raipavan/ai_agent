# Lounge & Waiting Area Furniture
Furniture for reception areas, waiting rooms, lounges and breakout zones.

## Product Range
- Reception sofas and 2-seater / 3-seater seating.
- Lounge chairs with comfortable upholstery.
- Waiting area benches and modular seating.
- Coffee tables and side tables to complete the lounge setup.

## Features to Explain
- Durable, easy-to-clean upholstery suited for high-traffic areas.
- Sturdy frames designed for daily commercial use.
- Modern and classic styles to match the office interior.
- Configurations for waiting areas of any size.

## Conversation Tips
- Ask about the space size and expected number of visitors to recommend the right configuration.
- Offer a site visit or layout guidance for larger waiting areas.
