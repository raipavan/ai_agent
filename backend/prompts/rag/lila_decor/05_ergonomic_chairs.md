# Ergonomic Chairs
Lila Decor's ergonomic chairs are built for all-day comfort in offices and work-from-office setups.

## Key Features to Explain
- Adjustable lumbar support to protect the lower back.
- Breathable mesh back options (E-Mesh) for air circulation.
- Height-adjustable gas lift and seat height range.
- Adjustable armrests (height and angle) on executive models.
- Tilt and recline mechanisms with tension control.
- Sturdy five-star bases with smooth casters.
- High-quality foam seating for long-session comfort.
- Weight capacity suited for daily commercial use (confirm exact spec with team).

## Conversation Tips
- If the customer mentions back pain or long working hours, recommend the mesh-back ergonomic models and mention lumbar support and adjustable features.
- If they mention senior staff or executives, recommend the imported executive series (Dextor, E-Mesh, Milton, Spider, Glider).
- Offer to send the catalog and price tiers via WhatsApp or email after the call.
