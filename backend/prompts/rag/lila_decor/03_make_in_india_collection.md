# Make in India Collection
The Make in India collection is Lila Decor's premium Indian craftsmanship line — built in our Mumbai facility with solid materials and excellent value.

## Highlights
- Premium Indian craftsmanship with attention to detail and finishing.
- Strong, durable frames built for daily commercial use.
- Competitive pricing — the cost-effective option when budget matters.
- Shorter lead times compared to imported products (made locally).
- Wide range: office chairs, desks, training seating, lounge furniture, cafeteria furniture.

## When to Offer
- Customer is budget conscious or price-sensitive.
- Customer needs durable furniture in bulk.
- Customer wants faster delivery.
- Mention it as the value option and offer the catalog with pricing tiers.
