# Product Categories
Lila Decor manufactures furniture across these main categories:

1. Office & ergonomic chairs — executive chairs, task chairs, visitor chairs, mesh-back chairs
2. Lounge & waiting area seating — reception sofas, lounge chairs, benches, waiting area seating
3. Training room furniture — training chairs, foldable/stackable seating, training tables, podium systems
4. Cafeteria / canteen furniture — dining tables, canteen chairs, stools, easy-clean surfaces
5. Office desks & workstations — modular workstations, conference tables, meeting room tables

## How to Guide the Customer
Ask which category they need first: office/ergonomic chairs, lounge or waiting area seating, training room furniture, or cafeteria/canteen furniture. Then ask quantity or seats needed, preferred style (modern, classic, ergonomic), delivery location and timeline.
