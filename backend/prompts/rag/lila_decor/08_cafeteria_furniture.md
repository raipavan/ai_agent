# Cafeteria / Canteen Furniture
Furniture for cafeterias, canteens, staff dining areas and food courts.

## Product Range
- Canteen dining tables (2-seater, 4-seater, 6-seater).
- Cafeteria chairs and stools.
- Bench systems for high-capacity dining.
- Easy-clean, scratch-resistant tabletops.

## Features to Explain
- Tough, easy-to-clean surfaces built for daily food service use.
- Sturdy, stable frames for high-traffic dining areas.
- Compact options for small staff canteens, and high-capacity layouts for large cafeterias.
- Table and chair combinations that fit the available space.

## Conversation Tips
- Ask the number of people dining and the space available to recommend capacity.
- Mention we can help with layout planning for canteens.
