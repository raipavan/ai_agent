# Customization, Warranty, Delivery & Site Visits
## Customization
- Bulk orders can be customized: fabric/color choices, corporate branding, special sizes and layouts.
- Confirm customization details with the team — never commit to custom work without checking availability.

## Warranty & Quality
- All Lila Decor furniture is manufactured to commercial-grade durability standards.
- After-sales support available for all collections, including the imported executive series.
- Warranty terms are shared by the team on request — never quote warranty terms you are not sure of.

## Delivery & Timelines
- Make in India collection: generally faster lead times (manufactured locally).
- Imported executive series: lead times confirmed with the team.
- Standard delivery estimates are confirmed by the team before any commitment.
- Urgent requirements are flagged to the team for a special confirmation.

## Site Visits & Consultation
- Site visits and consultations available for offices, training centers, cafeterias and lounges.
- During booking, gather: venue type, seating capacity, preferred style, delivery location.
- Availability is checked before confirming any slot; estimated timeline is given after confirmation.
- Details are sent via SMS, WhatsApp or email after booking.
