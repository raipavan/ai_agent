AC service — gas top-up, general service, compressor, condenser, cabin filter. Denting & painting — per panel, bumper, roof, full body, scratch repair, touch-up pen. Wheel services — alignment (2/4 wheel), balancing (per/4), rotation, alloy wheel. Brake services — pads front/rear, disc, drum, fluid, ABS sensor. Oil change — mineral / semi-synthetic / full synthetic / diesel (price incl. filter + labor). Battery — by Ah range, EFB/start-stop, old battery buyback deduction. Tyres — by size, brands stocked, price per tyre. Windshield & glass — front, rear, door glass, side mirror, crack repair. Annual maintenance contract (AMC) — all plans with coverage & price.
